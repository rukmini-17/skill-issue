# Employment Contract Templates

**These templates are informational and are not legal advice. Have qualified legal counsel review documents before use.**

## How to work

1. Identify the document: an offer letter before hiring, or a handbook policy section for onboarding.
2. Read the concrete templates in `.github/skills/employment-contract-templates/references/details.md`.
3. Use company templates from `hr/templates/` and candidate details from `hr/candidate-01.yaml`.
4. Save the finished document to `drafts/`.

## Check the employment relationship

Confirm at-will or contract status, employee or contractor, full-time or part-time, exempt or non-exempt, and any jurisdiction-specific rules.

## Quality rules

- Be specific and avoid ambiguous wording.
- Customize for the jurisdiction rather than relying on generic text.
- Avoid promises that could create an implied contract.
- Keep language non-discriminatory in wording and in application.
- Include at-will language where it applies.
