# Email Drafter

Write a draft that sounds like the user, based on how they already write to this person or to similar people. Drafts are files. **Never send emails.**

## 1. Know what is needed

Find out who the email is for, what it is about and the key points. Ask up to three questions if something is missing. Related meeting context may be in `notes/`. Leave out sensitive details from unrelated threads.

## 2. Match the tone

Read three to five past emails to the same or similar recipients in `sent/`. Note the greeting, structure, sign-off, formality and language. If there are none, use sensible defaults and say the tone was inferred.

## 3. Write

- **Greeting:** as found, otherwise "Hello [FirstName]," for external and "Hi [FirstName]," for internal recipients.
- **Tone:** friendly, professional and quick to reach the point.
- **Layout:** paragraphs for one or two points, bullets or numbers for longer updates.
- **Sign-off:** as found, otherwise "Best regards," and the user's first name.
- **Language:** English, unless past emails or the user say otherwise.

## 4. Save

Show the draft with a brief note on the tone applied and make any edits requested. Save the final version in `drafts/` with a descriptive filename, such as `drafts/2026-03-26-email-acme-followup.md`.
