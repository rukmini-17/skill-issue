# Alderpoint Freight Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days |
|------------------|-----------------|
| 0-2 years | 15 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

PTO accrues per pay period. Request PTO at least 2 weeks in advance. Manager approval is required.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave may be used for personal illness or family member care. A doctor's note is required for absences of more than 3 days.
